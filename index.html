About

I'm an identity engineer. I own Entra ID and the access around it: Azure, Intune, SharePoint, Atlassian, and 1Password. I also write the automation — Python, Django, Microsoft Graph, Logic Apps — so joiner / mover / leaver and access reviews are not a pile of spreadsheets.

Access control is what I started with in my computer science degree. In the cybersecurity master's I spent most of my time on IAM and GRC. SevenX was the first company where I ran that for real people: I owned access control and identity provisioning.

Hunt&Hackett hired me into the SOC. After a year of incidents where leftover access kept showing up, I moved to SecOps so I could own the identity systems instead of only seeing them in alerts. That is the work I want to keep doing.
