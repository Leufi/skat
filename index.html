How I got here

My dad runs a physical security company. Banks, big offices — places that still need people at the door. One day he came home looking miserable. A bank they were guarding had been hit by cyber fraud. Someone's account was compromised, the attackers moved through that identity, it turned into business email compromise, and a lot of money left the company. He said if they cannot protect what actually makes the company exist, he was not sure what the physical security was for.

I asked what would have helped. He said information security. That is when I started reading — how an account gets taken over, what identity has to do with it, what I would need to learn.

I was in telecom engineering. After that conversation it felt like the wrong path, so I stopped. I left with a diploma and went to India for computer science. That is where I first sat in a course called identity and access control. I got a scholarship for a cybersecurity master's and spent it on IAM and GRC.

SevenX was a part-time IT security analyst job while I was still in school — access, identity provisioning, audits, the human side of policy. In the Netherlands I did systemic design at Digital Society School. It is about making decisions with people, not at them, and saying the technical thing so a CEO can actually use it. That is GRC and IAM in human language.

Hunt&Hackett put me in the SOC. From June 2024 I split the week — three days SysOps, two days SOC — then moved fully to SysOps to build the identity layer, not only watch it. That is the work I want to keep doing.
